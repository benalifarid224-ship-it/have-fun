I want you to build a mobile application called "Have Fun".

IMPORTANT: Do not treat this as a generic event/social-media app. The core philosophy is:

"People are bored, they don't know what to do, and they don't know what is happening around them. Have Fun discovers activities and events and tells them about them. The goal is to get people OUT of the app and into real life."

The app should be designed for Android and iPhone.

==================================================
1. PRODUCT IDEA
==================================================

Have Fun is an event/activity discovery application.

Users can discover things happening around them in their country, starting with Tunisia.

Examples:
- Football matches
- Music concerts
- Festivals
- Parties
- Food events
- Art exhibitions
- Gaming events
- Car meets
- Drift events
- Outdoor activities
- Sports
- Cultural activities
- Student activities
- Shows
- Any interesting activity where people can go and have fun

The app should answer one simple question:

"I'm bored. What can I do?"

The app should quickly show:
- What is happening today
- What is happening tomorrow
- What is happening this week
- What is happening next month

The user should not need to spend a long time browsing.

==================================================
2. VERY IMPORTANT PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

DO NOT build this as a social network.

There is NO:
- Chat
- Direct messaging
- Complex friend system
- Feed designed to keep people scrolling
- Unnecessary social features

The app should NOT try to maximize time spent inside the app.

The desired user journey is:

EVENT DISCOVERED
       ↓
NOTIFICATION
       ↓
USER SEES THE EVENT
       ↓
USER OPENS EVENT
       ↓
USER GETS ALL IMPORTANT INFORMATION
       ↓
MAP / TICKET / EXTERNAL EVENT LINK
       ↓
USER LEAVES THE APP
       ↓
USER GOES HAVE FUN

The app is a bridge between people and real-world activities.
